Discusions

  1. How can teens protect their online privacy?

    • Teenagers can try to keep their online identity a secret by not sharing any personal information about themselves like their real names and address or what neighbourhood they live in. (Bruce, 2007)

    • They could also set up ground rules like not connecting to public Wi-Fi networks, not to accept friend requests from strangers, not to click on links or download anything, not to share personal information online. (Edwards, 2021)

  2. What are the challenges of online privacy?

    • Spying and snooping. When you are online you are spied by a number of trackers for various purposes like identity theft. Trackers keep a record of your search history and track all your online activities through various means. (Alexander, 2019)

  3. How can the challenges associate with online privacy addressed?

    • You can limit the personal information you share on social media, create strong passwords for your social media profiles to help prevent others from logging into them in your name and use different search engine and use a virtual private network like a VPN. (Rafter, 2021)

  4. What would be the best way to educate teens about online privacy?

    • Parents could spend time online with their children and explore different sites together and make their children aware of the dangers of interacting with strangers online. Share and discuss negative experiences when using the internet. (Lynn, 2017)

    • They could also advise them never to post hurtful or embarrassing messages online and use privacy settings. (Pearl, 2018)

  5. What impact does social media have on privacy?

    • The information gathered on social media can be used against you via blackmail or to impersonate you and also social media is a better, faster way to spread malicious content like scams and malware. (Wilson, 2019)

    • On social media there are many phishing attempts from cybercriminals that come in a form of an email, direct message, text, or phone call in an attempt to access your sensitive personal information. There can also botnet attacks for instance, a social media bot might be programmed to follow or respond to users who mention a certain keyword.(Hughes, 2021)

  6. Why are most teens so careless when it comes to their online privacy?

    • It's because they are still young and they couldn’t care less about their privacy that's why they post, share and like everything. (Henley, 2013)

  7. How many teens are still not aware about their online privacy?

    • 67% of internet users in the US are not aware of their country’s privacy and data protection rules. 9% of US internet users believe the concept of digital privacy is a myth.(Vuleta, 2021)

    • A recent survey shows that 38% of those polled had no idea how to secure their data. In fact 46% have no clue to begin safeguarding the sensitive information they provide the internet.(Galov, 2022)
